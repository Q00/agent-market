Shops

Deals count under this round's rule. Profit = each deal's price minus the item's floor (lamp 100, chair 180, bookshelf 260), the shop's cost. Revenue = the prices on those deals. Stars come from the reviews this round shows; the stars ranking needs 10 reviews. Click a shop to read the reviews buyers left for it.

Buyers

Paid = average price over the order's budget (lower is better); the best-price ranking needs 10 deals. Saved = budget left over, summed over deals. Held = orders whose budget was below the shop's floor that ended without a deal, out of all such orders.
